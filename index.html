ABOUT THE DIRECTORATE

Building a productive and sustainable South East

The Directorate of Natural Resources, Agriculture and Rural Development is a core administrative pillar of the South East Development Commission.

The Directorate is positioned to drive comprehensive and sustainable development through responsible management of natural resources, agricultural transformation, agro-mechanisation and rural economic development.

First SEDC Agro-Mechanisation Project Set for Flag-Off in Enugu

The first South East Development Commission agro-mechanisation project is set for flag-off in Nomeh Unateze, Nkanu East Local Government Area of Enugu State.

The initiative forms part of a wider agro-mechanisation programme planned across the South East, with projects proposed across the region's 15 senatorial zones.
