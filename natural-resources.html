Ebonyi Mineral Value Chain Acceleration Programme (EM-VCAP)

A targeted industrial framework engineered to maximize solid mineral endowments. EM-VCAP optimizes mining, refines processing ecosystems, and attracts high-ticket investments to build a thriving mineral value chain in Ebonyi State.

Key Engagements

  • September 2025: Participates in panels at the Intra-African Trade Fair (IATF), Algiers – investments in natural resources, digital gateways and strategic partnerships.
  • July 2026: Leads discussions with the National Biotechnology Development Agency on biotech innovations for economic growth linked to natural resources.
