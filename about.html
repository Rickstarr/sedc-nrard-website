About NRARD SEDC

The Directorate of Natural Resources, Agriculture and Rural Development is positioned to drive comprehensive and sustainable development across the South East region.

01 • Who We Are

Building a Sustainable South East

The Directorate of Natural Resources, Agriculture and Rural Development is a core administrative pillar of the South East Development Commission.

We are strategically positioned to drive comprehensive, sustainable development across the South East region. Our work centres on responsible natural resource management, agro-mechanisation and the economic transformation of rural communities.

Our Vision

To promote economic integration, environmental sustainability, food self-sufficiency and infrastructural modernization across the South East region.

Our People

The Directorate is led by the Executive Director of Natural Resources, Agriculture and Rural Development.

02 • What We Do

Our Core Areas of Work

Our work combines natural resource management, agricultural transformation and rural development to support sustainable economic growth across the South East.

Natural Resources

Promoting sustainable management and proactive conservation of natural ecosystems while leveraging resources to create wealth and support socio-economic development.

Agriculture

Supporting food security, agricultural productivity, value-chain development, climate-smart practices and stronger rural livelihoods.

Rural Development

Supporting human capital development, infrastructure and rural economic acceleration to help bridge the urban-rural divide.

Economic Value

Strengthening agricultural and natural-resource value chains while opening opportunities for investment, enterprise and commercial growth.

South East Agro-Transformation Programme

An integrated regional initiative designed to modernize the agricultural landscape through broad agro-mechanisation projects.

Key areas include Farm Estates, Livestock Hubs, Poultry Feed Processing Plants, Storage Facilities and targeted Agribusiness Development funding.

03 • Where We Work

Across the South East

The Directorate operates across all five South East states, with priority communities and project sites supporting agriculture, natural resources and rural development.

Abia

Farm estate partnerships with the Abia State Ministry of Agriculture, alongside assessments of oil palm and rubber estates and the 10,000-hectare Ofiavu plot in Bende LGA.

Anambra

More than 25,000 hectares were mapped, including rice clusters in Ayamelum and Umunze, Ugbene ATC and the Lower Anambra Irrigation Project in Omor.

Ebonyi

Community sensitisation under the Renewed Hope Community Advancement Programme in Ngbo, Ohaukwu LGA, alongside the Ebonyi Mineral Value Chain Acceleration Programme.

Enugu Image

Enugu

Activities include the Enugu Farm Settlement in Nkanu West, South East Vision 2050, agro-mechanisation engagements across Oji River, Uzo-Uwani and Nkanu East, and multiple institutional and agricultural events.

Imo

Activities include civic engagements in Ngor Okpala and Obiangwu, the Owerri roadmap disclosure, mapping at Numo Farms and Petros Farms, and the FUTO ElectroLab-X commissioning.
