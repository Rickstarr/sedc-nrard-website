South East Agro-Transformation Programme

An integrated regional initiative designed to modernize the agricultural landscape through broad agro-mechanization projects.

Key projects include state-of-the-art Farm Estates, scalable Livestock hubs, advanced Poultry Feed Processing plants, modern Storage Facilities, and targeted Agribusiness Development funding designed to accelerate youth employment.

Key Engagements

  • Mid-February 2025: Outlines three-phase strategy for agricultural revitalisation, job creation and economic reconstruction.
  • March 2025: Inspects 200-hectare Enugu State Farm Settlement (Nkanu West) – supports storage facilities, fishery development, food sufficiency and agrotourism.
  • July 2025: Receives high-level delegation from China Overseas Engineering Group (COVEC) to explore partnerships for large-scale farming, agro-processing and value-chain development.
  • September 2025: Participates in Intra-African Trade Fair (IATF), Algiers – agriculture investment panels, AfCFTA and strategic partnerships.
  • Late September 2025: Holds strategic meeting with NIRSAL MD/CEO to integrate agricultural development mandates with financing and innovation support.
  • Early December 2025: Leads SEDC delegation in partnership engagement with Abia State Ministry of Agriculture on farm estates across three senatorial zones.
  • December 2025: Completes field assessments of key Abia sites including oil palm/rubber estates and the 10,000-hectare Ofiavu plot, Bende LGA.
  • Late February 2026: Maps more than 25,000 hectares across Anambra agricultural zones for modern farm estates and agro-industrial hubs.
  • 13–14 April 2026: Chairs opening ceremony of IAEA/NRCRI Regional Training Course on Climate-Smart Agriculture for cassava production.
  • May 2026: Conducts facility mappings at Numo Farms Ltd and Petros Farms (Imo) and engages Ubichukwu Autonomous Community to strengthen the Agro-Mechanisation Programme.
  • July 2026: Holds discussions with National Biotechnology Development Agency on biotech innovations for agricultural development.
  • July 2026: Leads community stakeholder engagements across Enugu’s three senatorial zones — Uzo-Uwani, Oji River and Nkanu East — ahead of Agro-Mechanisation project flag-off.
  • 1–4 September 2026: Attends 20th Africa Food Systems Forum, Kigali – canvasses investments in South East agri-food systems, starting with Enugu State.
  • Early–Mid September 2026: Delivers keynote at Eastern International Poultry Summit 2026, Enugu – prioritises poultry industry rebuilding for food security and jobs.
