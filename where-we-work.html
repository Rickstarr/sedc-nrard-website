Where We Work

The Directorate operates across all five South East states, with priority communities and project sites identified for strategic agricultural, agro-industrial, infrastructure and community development initiatives.

South East Nigeria

Working Across the Five South East States

Our work is rooted in local communities and strategic project locations across Abia, Anambra, Ebonyi, Enugu and Imo States. Through partnerships, field assessments, community engagements and project mapping, the Directorate identifies opportunities that can contribute to sustainable development and economic transformation.

Priority Locations

Our Project Sites & Communities

Abia

Ibeku
  • December 2025: Partnership with the Abia State Ministry of Agriculture on farm estates.
  • Field assessments of oil palm and rubber estates.
  • Assessment of the 10,000-hectare Ofiavu plot in Bende LGA.

Anambra

Ayamelum • Umunze • Omor
  • February 2026: Comprehensive mapping of more than 25,000 hectares.
  • Rice clusters in Ayamelum and Umunze.
  • Ugbene ATC and the Lower Anambra Irrigation Project in Omor.
  • Mapping supports plans for modern farm estates and agro-industrial hubs.

Ebonyi

Ngbo • Ohaukwu LGA
  • September 2026: Renewed Hope Community Advancement Programme sensitisation in Ngbo.
  • Community engagement in Ohaukwu LGA.
  • Focus on the EM-VCAP and mineral value-chain development.

Enugu

Oji River • Uzo-Uwani • Nkanu East
  • March 2025: Enugu Farm Settlement initiative in Nkanu West.
  • February 2026: Vision 2050 convening.
  • July 2026: Agro-mechanisation community engagements across three senatorial zones.
  • Poultry Summit and multiple institutional events.

Imo

Ehime Mbano • Ngor Okpala • Obiangwu
  • January & May 2025: Ngor Okpala and Obiangwu civic engagements.
  • May 2025: Owerri roadmap disclosure.
  • May 2026: Numo Farms and Petros Farms mapping.
  • April 2026: FUTO ElectroLab-X commissioning.

Connecting Communities to Development Opportunities

By working directly with communities, state institutions, development partners and other stakeholders, the Directorate seeks to identify opportunities and support initiatives that strengthen agriculture, agro-processing, mineral value chains, infrastructure and inclusive economic development across the South East.

The Directorate works to support development, agricultural transformation, agro-industrial growth and community advancement across the South East.
