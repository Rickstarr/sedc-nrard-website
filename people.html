Dr Cliff Chimeremeze Ogbede

Dr Cliff Chimeremeze Ogbede is the Executive Director of Natural Resources, Agriculture and Rural Development. He was sworn in on the 11th of February 2025 alongside other Executive Directors.

Institutional Timeline

Early January 2025
Hosts Ngor Okpala Journalists Forum; articulates SEDC vision and pledges service with integrity for regional rehabilitation and agricultural revitalisation.
13–15 January 2025
Screened and confirmed by the Senate Ad Hoc Committee on Regional Development Commissions (chaired by Senator Orji Uzor Kalu).
11 February 2025
Officially inaugurated as Executive Director following Board inauguration by President Bola Tinubu.
Mid-February 2025
Outlines three-phase strategy (quick wins, mid-term, long-term) for agricultural revitalisation, job creation and economic reconstruction.
Early February 2026
Participates in the SEDC-hosted inaugural convening for South East Vision 2050 – a 25-year regional blueprint.
