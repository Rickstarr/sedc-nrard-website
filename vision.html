Our Vision

Our Vision

The Directorate is strategically positioned to drive comprehensive, sustainable development across the South East region.

Its work promotes economic integration, environmental sustainability, food self-sufficiency and infrastructural modernization of the South East region.

The development approach centres on responsible natural-resource management, agro-mechanization and the economic transformation of rural communities.
